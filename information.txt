Welcome to the 2025 cragmas guesser!

Every day a picture of the admin's archive is published, depicting a climber on a route.
Guess the grade of the route and try to become the Cragmas-Champion 2025.

Each correct guess is rewarded with 3 points.
A deviation of one grade will be rewarded with 2 points.
A deviation of two grades from the true grade will be rewarded with 1 point.
The Leaderboard is always delayed by one day to keep the grades secret for the whole day.

You can only guess once per day and only for the current day, so make sure to check the calendar every day.

BE FAIR: only use your own name/gamertag. 
This website doesn't use fancy login and security so don't make guesses in the name of someone else.
